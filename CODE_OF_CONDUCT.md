# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Pliego Lab to be respectful and safe for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, origin, language, disability, appearance, orientation, or any other characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others respectfully and collaborate in good faith.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas, code, and observable behavior.
- Provide clear explanations for people who are new to the project.
- Accept corrections and remain willing to learn.
- Respect the privacy of chats, characters, images, and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information or credentials.
- Unsolicited sexual content, especially in issues or public spaces.
- Disrupting discussions, spamming, or filing deliberately malicious reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or close content that violates this code and may limit the participation of repeat offenders. Reports should be made privately through `SECURITY.md` when they involve harassment, personal data, or a vulnerability.

This code is inspired by Contributor Covenant 2.1.
